# ❄️ SubZero

### Your subscriptions. Under control.

**SubZero** is a cross-platform subscription management application designed to help people organise recurring expenses, track payment dates, monitor free trials and understand their spending — all in one place.

The goal is simple: make managing subscriptions effortless.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Subscriptions have become part of everyday life. Streaming platforms, cloud storage, productivity tools and countless other services create recurring payments that are easy to forget.

SubZero aims to bring all these commitments into one simple, organised dashboard.

## ✨ Features

🚧 Features are planned or under development unless explicitly marked as completed.

- 📊 **Spending Dashboard** – Understand monthly and yearly subscription expenses.
- 💳 **Subscription Manager** – Add, edit, organise and manage subscriptions.
- 📅 **Payment Calendar** – Track upcoming recurring payments.
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** – Receive reminders before payment dates.
- ⏳ **Free Trial Tracker** – Keep track of trial periods and expiration dates.
- 📈 **Spending Analytics** – Explore spending patterns and categories.
- 🧮 **What-If Calculator** – Estimate how changing subscriptions affects recurring expenses.
- 🌍 **Multi-Currency Support** – Manage subscriptions in different currencies.
- 🔐 **Privacy First** – Keep personal financial information protected.
