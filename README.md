**SubZero** is a modern, privacy-first cross-platform **subscription management application** designed to help people organize recurring expenses, track payment dates, monitor free trials, and understand their spending—all in one place. Built with Flutter, SubZero acts as your ultimate expense tracker, ensuring you never pay for an unwanted subscription again.

The goal is simple: make managing personal finances and recurring subscriptions completely effortless.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Subscriptions have become a major part of everyday life. Streaming platforms, cloud storage, productivity tools, and countless other services create recurring payments that are easy to forget, leading to unwanted charges.

SubZero aims to bring all these financial commitments into one simple, beautifully designed, and highly organized dashboard. Take control of your money, cancel unused services, and plan your budget better.

## ✨ Features

🚧 *Features are planned or under active development unless explicitly marked as completed.*

- 📊 **Spending Dashboard** – Instantly understand your monthly and yearly subscription expenses.
- 💳 **Subscription Manager** – Easily add, edit, organize, and manage subscriptions in one place.
- 📅 **Payment Calendar** – Track upcoming recurring payments visually so you are never surprised.
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** – Receive timely push notifications before payment dates.
- ⏳ **Free Trial Tracker** – Keep track of trial periods and expiration dates to cancel before being charged.
- 📈 **Spending Analytics** – Explore spending patterns, filter by categories, and optimize your budget.
- 🧮 **What-If Calculator** – Estimate how adding or canceling subscriptions affects your recurring expenses.
- 🌍 **Multi-Currency Support** – Manage international subscriptions with real-time conversion.
- 🔐 **Privacy First** – Your personal financial information stays protected and local by default.

## 🚀 Project Vision

SubZero is being developed with long-term ambitions to become the ultimate personal finance hub for subscription tracking.

Future possibilities include:
- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Family Subscription Management**: Track and split costs across family members.
- 🧠 **Advanced Spending Intelligence**: AI-powered recommendations to save money.
- ☁️ **Cloud Synchronization**: Access your data seamlessly across all devices.
- 🏠 **Shared Household Expenses**: Manage internet, utilities, and joint subscriptions.
- 📉 **Subscription Price Monitoring**: Alerts when a service increases its pricing.
- 💡 **Personalized Spending Insights**: Deep dive into where your money goes every month.

The vision is to grow SubZero beyond a simple tracker into a comprehensive recurring-expense management platform.
